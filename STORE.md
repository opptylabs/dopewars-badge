## Description (2000 characters max)

The classic trading game, ported from the Android app and moved to London for Breakpoint 2026.

You start at Olympia with $2,000 and a $5,500 debt growing 10% a day. Ride the Tube between six stations, from Olympia to King's Cross and Liverpool Street: every trip takes a day and brings new prices. Buy low, sell high, and get rich in 30 days.

Watch out for busts, muggers, police dogs and three cops, each tougher than the last. Run or fight.

Original game by John E. Dell, 1984. Also on Android and the App Store.
